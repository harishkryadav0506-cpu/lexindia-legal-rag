### Authority Levels
- **Level 1 (Statute)**: Acts passed by Parliament (e.g., Income Tax Act 1961, Finance Acts)
- **Level 2 (Rules)**: Subordinate legislation framed by CBDT/Ministry (e.g., Income Tax Rules 1962)
- **Level 3 (Circulars / Notifications)**: CBDT administrative clarifications and statutory notifications
- **Level 4 (Instructions / Guidelines)**: Departmental filing instructions (e.g., ITR-1 to ITR-4 instructions)
